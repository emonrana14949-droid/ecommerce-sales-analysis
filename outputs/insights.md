# Key Insights — E-commerce Sales Analysis

> This project uses synthetic practice data generated with NumPy. The patterns below are for learning the workflow only; they are not real business findings.

- Dataset size: **600 orders** across 2025.
- Total revenue in the dataset: **12,422,273.73 BDT**.
- The best month by revenue is **month 7** with **1,513,209.77 BDT**.
- The top product category by revenue is **Electronics** (8,389,877.75 BDT from 152 orders).
- The top city by revenue is **Dhaka** (5,428,932.84 BDT).
- Correlation between discount percentage and revenue: **-0.12**.
